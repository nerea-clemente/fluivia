Accounting and tax advisory firms

Collecting documents

You ask each client for invoices and receipts one by one, by email and by phone.

We send the reminders for you and each client uploads their documents to their own folder.

Repeated questions

You answer the same questions about deadlines, tax forms and documents again and again.

An assistant drafts the reply using your criteria, and you check it before it goes out.

Tax calendar

Each client's deadlines are in separate spreadsheets.

You see them all on one dashboard, with an alert before each date.

Estate agencies

Leads from property portals

You copy the contacts from each property portal by hand.

They go straight into your list, sorted by property and area.

First reply

Requests for information or viewings get answered late.

The person gets the property details and viewing times straight away.

Listings and adverts

You write every property description from scratch.

We draft the advert from the property spec sheet and you tweak it.

Clinics

Appointments and empty slots

You pick up the phone in the middle of sessions, and slots from cancellations stay empty.

Patients book online, confirm from the reminder, and free slots are offered to the waiting list.

A first visit without paperwork

On arrival, the patient fills in the form, the consent and the data protection notice on paper.

They fill it in and sign it on their phone before they come, and the practitioner gets a summary.

Follow-up and reviews

Some patients drop their treatment halfway, and happy patients never leave a review.

Exercises and reminders arrive on their phone, and we ask for a review when they are discharged.

Wholesale and distribution

Orders through several channels

Orders come in by email, phone and WhatsApp and have to be typed in by hand.

They are all logged in the same place and in the same format.

Delivery status

Customers call to ask where their order is.

We let them know when the order ships and when it is delivered.

Stock and reordering

You check stock by eye and order from the supplier once something has already run out.

You get an alert when a product drops below the minimum, with a suggested order for you to confirm.

Tutoring and language schools

Enrolment

Sign-ups arrive on paper or by email and then get copied into a spreadsheet.

Families sign up through a form that goes straight to your student list and payments.

Questions from families and students

Every week people ask the same things about timetables, prices and groups.

An assistant answers with the school's information and passes the rest on to you.

Notices and reminders

You send class changes and payment reminders one message at a time.

Notices go out scheduled by group and you can see who has received them.

Consultancies and agencies

Onboarding new clients

You send the same emails, documents and logins to every new client.

When they sign, the welcome emails and logins go out on their own.

Proposals and documents

You rebuild proposals and reports from old files.

We prepare templates and drafts with the information from each project.

Internal knowledge

In every meeting someone asks where that document was.

Your team finds it by asking in their own words, as they would ask a colleague.

Shops

Customer messages

People ask about opening hours, stock and delivery at all hours.

They get answers with your shop's information, and we let you know when a person needs to step in.

Reservations and orders

Orders are jotted down in a notebook or on your phone.

They are logged in one place and the customer gets a message when their order is ready.

Suppliers and invoices

You have invoices in your inbox, on paper and on your desk.

They are collected and sorted automatically, ready to pass on to your accountant.
