What we do with your data

We work with enquiries, documents and your clients' data, so on every project we commit to the following.

Data processing agreement

We sign a data processing agreement with you, as GDPR requires, listing the tools involved.

Professional tools

We use business versions, where your data isn't used to train AI models, and never personal accounts.

EU servers wherever possible

We choose providers that keep data in Europe. If there's no other option, we only use those that offer the safeguards GDPR requires.

Only the data that's needed

The AI only gets the information it needs for each task, and everything else stays in your systems.

Secure access in your name

Accounts are in your company's name, with two-step verification and only the permissions needed. When we finish, we return the data to you or delete it.

Transparent AI

Assistants that talk to your clients say they're an AI, and there's always a way to reach a person.

Decisions about people, made by people

We don't build systems that score or reject candidates, clients or employees. The AI can sort the information, but you decide.

We explain it in writing

On every project we give you a summary of which data is used, where it's stored, who has access and what happens to it at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Got another question? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

What if what I need isn't on the website?

That happens a lot, because the website only shows a few examples. Tell us which task takes up your time and we'll tell you if we can solve it, by connecting your programs or building something to measure, like a booking diary or a portal for your clients.

Do I need to know about technology or AI?

No. Something like "I waste a lot of time answering the same questions" is enough. We find the tool and explain it with examples.

Do I have to change the programs I already use?

Usually not. First we try to connect what you already use, like email, spreadsheets or your management software. We only suggest something new if it will save you work.

Will the AI reply to my clients on its own?

Only if you want it to. Often the most useful set-up is for the AI to draft a reply or sort the information and for someone on your team to check it. We always tell you which part a person still has to do.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what's included. Each project has a fixed price that we agree before we start. We also run diagnostic and training sessions, and offer maintenance if you want it.

How long until something is up and running?

We start with one specific task so you notice the difference soon. We put the timeline in the proposal, depending on what needs to be set up and tested.

What happens to my company's and my clients' data?

We handle it in line with GDPR. We sign a data processing agreement, use business tools that don't train on your data and choose servers in the EU. We also explain in writing where the information is stored and who has access. See our commitments.

Can the AI make decisions for me?

No. The AI drafts, sorts information and flags what's worth checking, but important decisions, especially those that affect people, are always made by someone on your team.

Which task would you like to get off your plate?

Tell us about it and we'll let you know if we can help. The first conversation doesn't commit you to anything.
