Privacy policy

This policy explains how we process the personal data you give us through this website, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Danish Data Protection Act (databeskyttelsesloven).

Data controller

  • Controller: CLEMPA & Co.
  • CVR (Danish company number): 43172794
  • Address: Agerbæksvej 32, 1. 2., 8240 Risskov (Aarhus), Denmark
  • Email: info@fluivia.ai

Fluivia is the trade name under which CLEMPA & Co. provides these services.

What data we process and why

When you write to us through the contact form, we process your name, company, email address, phone number (if you give it), industry and the content of your message, for the sole purpose of answering your enquiry and, where relevant, preparing a proposal.

When you request a demo, we process your name, your email address, your business name and your industry in order to send you the personalised demo and, afterwards, to write to you to ask whether you found it useful. We don't add you to any mailing list without your permission.

Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form, and on taking pre-contractual steps at your request.

Retention

We keep the data for as long as needed to deal with your request and, after that, for the periods required by law.

Recipients

We don't pass your data on to third parties unless required by law. We use providers that process it on our behalf: Netlify (website hosting and forms), Cloudflare (contact management, backups in the EU and email) and Resend (sending emails). Some are based in the United States; in that case, the transfer takes place with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to info@fluivia.ai. You can also lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, www.datatilsynet.dk) or the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Cookies

This website does not use its own or third-party cookies for analytics or advertising. If any are added in the future, you will be informed through the corresponding notice.
