What's included

01

Quarterly paperwork

Each client gets a list of what they need to send for their case and a link to their area, where they upload it from their phone or computer.

Reminders go out on their own and only ask for what's missing. You can see at a glance who has sent everything and who you need to call.

02

Invoice reading

Invoices and receipts are read as they arrive, and an entry is suggested with the date, supplier, net amount and VAT.

Your team only checks the ones that raise doubts, and the data is prepared in the format your accounting software imports.

03

Client questions

An assistant answers on WhatsApp or by email about deadlines, outstanding documents and tax forms, using the firm's details and each client's.

It doesn't answer anything that needs advice. It passes that on to your team with a summary of what the client is asking.

04

New clients

A new client fills in a form that asks for what their case needs, like their ID card (DNI), their registration with the Spanish Tax Agency or the premises lease.

Documents are checked as they're uploaded, so the file reaches you complete and nobody has to write three emails asking for what's missing.

What would the client area look like?

It carries your firm's name and branding, and it opens in the browser, with nothing to download.

On the client's phone

  • Sees which documents are missing
  • Uploads invoices and receipts with a photo
  • Checks their upcoming deadlines
  • Sees how their case is going

On your team's computer

  • Who has sent everything and who hasn't
  • The invoices read and ready to check
  • The questions that need advice

Frequently asked questions

Can it connect to A3, Sage or Holded?

Yes. If your software allows a direct connection, we use that. If not, we prepare the invoice data in its import format so you can load it in one go.

Does the AI do the bookkeeping on its own?

No. It reads the invoices and suggests the entry, but someone on your team reviews the unclear ones and nothing is posted unless you decide so.

What happens to my clients' data?

We sign a data processing agreement with you. The data is stored on EU servers and the AI tools we use don't train on it.

What about invoicing under Verifactu (Spain's e-invoicing rules)?

Invoices are still issued with certified software. We automate how your clients' documents are collected, read and organised.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we include. As a guide, document collection with invoice reading starts at €990 to set up and €99 a month, and the enquiries assistant at €690 and €89 a month. The client area is built to measure. Prices exclude VAT, and the proposal also sets out licences and maintenance.

Who helps me if something goes wrong?

We do. Write to us at info@fluivia.ai and we look at it the same working day. The monthly fee includes maintenance and small adjustments.

How do you ask clients for their documents now?

Tell us what you use and what you'd like to improve. If you like, we can talk it through on a call and tell you what would make sense for your firm.
