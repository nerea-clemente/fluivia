What takes up the most time in an accountancy firm

Quarterly paperwork

You message each client one by one and keep an Excel sheet of who has sent what.

Each client gets their checklist and a link to their area, and the reminders only ask for what's missing.

Invoices in a thousand formats

Someone types photos of receipts and PDFs into the accounting software.

Each invoice is read as it arrives and an entry is suggested. Your team only checks the ones that raise doubts.

Repeated questions

Clients ask on WhatsApp, at any hour, "when's the VAT deadline?".

An assistant answers each client's deadlines and details, and passes anything that needs advice on to you.

New clients

It takes several emails to gather the ID card (DNI), the registration with the Spanish Tax Agency or the premises lease, and something is always missing.

The client fills in a form that asks for what their case needs and checks the documents as they upload them.

Frequently asked questions

Got another question? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

Can it connect to A3, Sage or Holded?

Yes. If your software allows a direct connection, we use that. If not, we prepare the invoice data in its import format so you can load it in one go.

Does the AI do the bookkeeping on its own?

No. It reads the invoices and suggests the entry, but someone on your team reviews the unclear ones and nothing is posted unless you decide so.

What happens to my clients' data?

We sign a data processing agreement with you. The data is stored on EU servers and the AI tools we use don't train on it.

What about invoicing under Verifactu (Spain's e-invoicing rules)?

Invoices are still issued with certified software. We automate how your clients' documents are collected, read and organised.
