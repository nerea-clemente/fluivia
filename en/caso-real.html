Case study: Study and Beyond

Study and Beyond supports Spanish students who want to take a degree in Denmark. We built their entire working system, just as we would for your business.

A small team with a lot to coordinate

Every student needs months of support. They have to choose a university, prepare the application, meet the deadlines and gather documents that are checked one by one. Meanwhile, families have questions, new contacts arrive from the website and the social media posts have to keep going out.

By hand, all that meant scattered WhatsApp chats, folders, spreadsheets and reminders someone had to keep in their head. We wanted the team to spend their time with students rather than chasing paperwork or copying data from one place to another.

What we built

It's several connected parts that are used every day. For each one, we've added the name of the Fluivia service.

Student area

Each student signs in with a code sent to their email. They see their tasks step by step, upload documents and we tell them if they're fine or what needs changing. Deadline reminders reach them on their own, and the family gets an update each time they move on to a new stage.

At Fluivia: Client portal and Documents without chasing

Contacts and follow-up

Whatever comes in through the website forms goes in on its own, with no duplicates. Each contact has a stage, a next step and an alert when it's time to write to them, with email templates sent from the company's own domain.

At Fluivia: Custom CRM

Automatic emails and newsletter

Anyone who downloads a guide gets a few emails written for that moment. Newsletters are scheduled by group, and you can see the opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes, which are handled automatically.

At Fluivia: Email marketing and lead generation

Scheduled social media

Instagram and Facebook posts go out on their own four days a week through the official tools. Anything that can't be automated, like some stories, arrives as a reminder to post by hand.

At Fluivia: Social media automation

Digital office

An internal dashboard with the contacts, emails, content, documents and finances. When you log in, a summary tells you what has happened and what's due today. Expenses are logged with a photo of the receipt and exported for the accountancy firm.

At Fluivia: Digital office and expense tracking

Website and documents

A website with more than 70 pages covering +55 degrees, forms and legal texts. PDF guides and presentations come out in the brand's design, without laying them out by hand.

At Fluivia: Website design

How it's built

Data in Europe

The database and files are stored on servers in the European Union.

Secure access

You sign in with a code sent to each person's email, with no passwords written on a sticky note.

Backups and logs

There's a daily backup and a record of who does what, in case anything needs checking.

Built to measure

It isn't a generic program forced to fit. It does what the team needs and nothing more.

Cheap to run

It runs on cloud services that cost a few euros a month.

AI is only used where it helps. Anything that can be done with simple rules is done with rules, because it's cheaper and fails less.
