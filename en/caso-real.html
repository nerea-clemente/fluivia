Case study: Study and Beyond

Study and Beyond supports Spanish students who want to take a degree in Denmark. We built their entire working system, the same way we would build it for your business.

A small team with a lot to coordinate.

Every student needs months of support: choosing a university, preparing the application, meeting deadlines that won't wait and gathering documents that have to be checked one by one. Meanwhile, families have questions, new contacts arrive from the website and social media can't stop.

Done by hand, that means scattered WhatsApp chats, folders, spreadsheets and reminders kept in someone's head. What we wanted was for the team to spend their time on students, not chasing paperwork or copying data from one place to another.

What we built.

Pieces that work together and run every day. Next to each one, its name at Fluivia.

Student area

Each student signs in with a code sent to their email. They see their tasks step by step, upload documents and get them reviewed: accepted, or rejected with a note on what to change. Deadline reminders go out on their own and the family gets an update at each milestone.

At Fluivia: Client portal and Documents without chasing

Contacts and follow-up

Website forms come in on their own, without duplicating anyone. Each contact has a stage, a next step and a "your turn" alert when it's time to write to them, with email templates sent from their own domain.

At Fluivia: Custom CRM

Automatic emails and newsletter

Anyone who downloads a guide gets a series of emails written for that moment. Newsletters are scheduled by segment, with opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes all visible, and handled automatically.

At Fluivia: Email marketing and lead generation

Scheduled social media

Instagram and Facebook posts go out on their own through the official tools, four days a week. Anything that can't be automated, like some stories, arrives as a reminder to post by hand.

At Fluivia: Social media automation

Digital office

A single internal dashboard with contacts, emails, content, documents and finances. When you log in, a summary tells you what has happened and what's due today. Expenses are logged with a photo of the receipt and exported for the accountancy firm.

At Fluivia: Digital office and expense tracking

Website and documents

A website with more than 70 pages covering +55 degrees, forms and legal texts. PDF guides and presentations are generated in the brand's design, without laying out each one by hand.
