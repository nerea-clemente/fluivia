Frequently asked questions.

Got another question? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

Does it work with leads from Idealista and Fotocasa?

Yes. Alerts from these Spanish property portals arrive at an email address; from there they're read, answered and passed into your CRM without typing anything.

Does the client know an assistant is replying?

Yes. The assistant introduces itself as one and, as soon as it's needed, hands the conversation over to an agent.

Can it value properties?

It can give a rough price range based on recent sales in the area, which helps win over the owner. The asking price is always set by the agent after seeing the property.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

To send WhatsApp messages automatically, yes: it uses the official business version. We'll help you set it up.
