What's included

01

Replies to portal leads

When someone writes through Idealista or Fotocasa, we reply within a minute with the property details and a couple of questions to find out what they're looking for, at night and at weekends too.

The contact goes into your CRM without anyone typing it, and the agent receives it with their answers and a suggested viewing.

02

Viewings

The buyer picks a time from the agent's free slots and gets the confirmation and a reminder on the day.

After the viewing we ask what they thought, and the answer is saved on their record and on the property.

03

Reports for sellers

Every week the seller gets a report with the viewings, the interested buyers and what buyers mention most.

That gives you data to talk about the price, and sellers ask "how's it going with mine?" far less often.

04

Paperwork up to the notary

Each sale has a record of what's missing, like the land registry extract, the energy certificate or the mortgage papers, and who needs to bring it.

We send reminders to whoever needs them until signing, and your team sees what stage each sale is at.

What would the dashboard look like?

It carries your agency's name and branding. Your team uses it on a computer or phone, and clients don't have to download anything.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with leads from Idealista and Fotocasa?

Yes. Alerts from these Spanish property portals arrive at an email address, and from there they're read, answered and passed into your CRM without typing anything.

Does the client know an assistant is replying?

Yes. The assistant introduces itself as one and, as soon as it's needed, hands the conversation over to an agent.

Can it value properties?

It can give a rough price range based on recent sales in the area, which is useful for your first conversation with the owner. The asking price is always set by the agent after seeing the property.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

To send WhatsApp messages automatically, yes, because it uses the official business version. We'll help you set it up.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we include. As a guide, replies to portal leads start at €690 to set up and €89 a month, and the viewing diary at €390 and €49 a month. The CRM and the reports for sellers are built to measure. Prices exclude VAT, and the proposal also sets out licences and maintenance.

Who helps me if something goes wrong?

We do. Write to us at info@fluivia.ai and we look at it the same working day. The monthly fee includes maintenance and small adjustments.

How do you handle portal leads now?

Tell us what you use and what you'd like to improve. If you like, we can talk it through on a call and tell you what would make sense for your agency.
