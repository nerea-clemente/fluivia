Fluivia for schools

Enrolment, notices and renewals in one portal

We set up a portal so families can enrol on their phone and get your notices. You have the groups, attendance and renewals in one place.

Tell us what you need See the demo for a school

What's included

01

Online enrolment

Families enrol on their phone with a short placement test, and we recommend a group with free places.

In the same step they sign the rules and leave their payment details, and you see each group with its places up to date.

02

Questions from families

An assistant answers questions about timetables, places and bank holidays with your real groups, and offers a trial class to anyone who asks.

Anything a person needs to see goes to management with a summary.

03

Attendance and notices

The teacher takes the register on their phone and, if a student misses class, the family gets an alert with the day's homework.

We also send class changes and payment reminders by group, and you can see who has received them.

04

Renewals

In May each family gets the proposed group for next year and confirms their place or tells us why they are leaving.

In June you already know how many groups you'll have and what to change.

What would the portal look like?

It carries your school's name and branding, and it opens in the browser, with nothing to download.

On the family's or student's phone

  • Enrolment and the placement test
  • Absence alerts and homework
  • Notices from the school
  • Their place for next year

On your team's computer

  • The groups with their places
  • Each student's attendance
  • Questions from families
  • Who's renewing and who isn't

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for a small school?

Yes. We start with what takes up most of your time, which is usually enrolment or families' questions, and add the rest once it works.

Do families have to install anything?

No. Everything works from the phone's browser, on WhatsApp or by email.

What about children's data?

We ask families for the consents needed, for photos for example, and the data is stored in the EU with access limited to those who need it.

Does it connect to my management software?

If your software allows it, yes. If not, you can manage students and groups in the portal.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we include. As a guide, the enquiries assistant starts at €690 to set up and €89 a month, and notices and renewals at €490 plus the monthly fee. The portal with enrolment is built to measure. Prices exclude VAT, and the proposal also sets out licences and maintenance.

Who helps me if something goes wrong?

We do. Write to us at info@fluivia.ai and we look at it the same working day. The monthly fee includes maintenance and small adjustments.

How do you handle enrolment now?

Tell us what you use and what you'd like to improve. If you like, we can talk it through on a call and tell you what would make sense for your school.
