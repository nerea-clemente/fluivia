What takes up the most time at a distributor

Orders on WhatsApp

Every morning someone types messages, voice notes and photos of lists into the management software.

Each order is read, turned into lines with your product codes, checked against stock and entered into the software on its own.

Paper delivery notes

They're signed in the van and come back crumpled, or don't come back at all, and problems aren't recorded.

The customer signs on the driver's phone and any problems are saved with a photo.

"What time will you get here?"

Customers call the office and the office calls the driver.

The day before, we send the customer their delivery window, and we let them know when the delivery is on its way or running late.

Payments that drag on

Some overdue invoices never get chased because it's awkward or there's no time.

We send friendly reminders that get gradually firmer, and the delicate cases are left to the accounts team.

Frequently asked questions

Got another question? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

Does it work with my ERP?

With most of them. If it allows a direct connection, we connect it. If not, we prepare an import file or work with your order sheet.

What if the customer orders with a voice note or a photo of a list?

That can be read too. Anything that isn't clear is flagged for someone to check before the order is prepared.

Do the drivers need another phone?

No. It's a web app that runs on the phone they already have, with nothing to install.

Does it issue invoices?

No. Invoices are still issued with your certified invoicing software, and we take care of reminding customers about outstanding payments.
