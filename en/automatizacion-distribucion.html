Frequently asked questions.

Got another question? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

Does it work with my ERP?

With most of them. If it has a direct connection we use it; if not, we generate an import file or work with your order sheet.

What if the customer orders with a voice note or a photo of a list?

That can be read too. Anything that isn't understood with certainty is flagged for someone to check before the order is prepared.

Do the drivers need another phone?

No. It's a web app that runs on the phone they already have, with nothing to install.

Does it issue invoices?

No. Invoices are still issued with your certified invoicing software; we take care of reminding customers about outstanding payments.
