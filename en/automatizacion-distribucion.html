Fluivia for distribution

WhatsApp orders, straight into your management software

We set up a system that moves orders from WhatsApp and email into your management software, collects delivery note signatures on the phone and reminds customers about outstanding payments. Your team sees every order, every delivery and what each customer owes in one place.

What's included

01

Orders that enter themselves

Orders that come in on WhatsApp or by email, including voice notes and photos of lists, are read and turned into lines with your product codes.

Stock is checked and they're loaded into your management software. Anything that isn't clear is flagged for someone to check before the order is prepared.

02

Delivery notes on the phone

The driver shows the delivery note on their phone and the customer signs with a finger. If a case is missing or broken, it's logged with a photo.

The signed note reaches the office straight away, and any problems are saved for the credit note.

03

Delivery updates

The day before, each customer gets their delivery window, and on the day we let them know when the van is on its way.

If there is a delay, they get an update, and the office stops getting calls asking what time you will arrive.

04

Payment reminders

When an invoice is overdue, we send a friendly reminder with the invoice attached and, if it's still unpaid, a firmer one.

A dashboard shows you what each customer owes and how they've replied. The delicate cases are left to the accounts team.

What would the dashboard look like?

It carries your company's name and branding. The office uses it on a computer, drivers on their phone, and nobody has to install anything.

On the driver's phone

  • The day's route
  • Delivery notes to sign
  • Problems logged with a photo
  • Updates to each customer

On the office computer

  • The orders that have come in
  • The ones that need checking
  • The day's deliveries
  • Outstanding payments

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with my ERP?

With most of them. If it allows a direct connection, we connect it. If not, we prepare an import file or work with your order sheet.

What if the customer orders with a voice note or a photo of a list?

That can be read too. Anything that isn't clear is flagged for someone to check before the order is prepared.

Do the drivers need another phone?

No. It's a web app that runs on the phone they already have, with nothing to install.

Does it issue invoices?

No. Invoices are still issued with your certified invoicing software, and we take care of reminding customers about outstanding payments.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we include and on your management software. As a guide, payment reminders start at €490 to set up plus the monthly fee. Automatic orders and delivery notes on the phone are built to measure, depending on your ERP. Prices exclude VAT, and the proposal also sets out licences and maintenance.

Who helps me if something goes wrong?

We do. Write to us at info@fluivia.ai and we look at it the same working day. The monthly fee includes maintenance and small adjustments.

How do orders reach you now?

Tell us what you use and what you'd like to improve. If you like, we can talk it through on a call and tell you what would make sense for your company.
