Marketing

So people find you and get in touch without your website and social media eating your hours. We automate the repetitive parts and you decide what gets published.

01

Social media automation

We prepare a calendar and drafts for each network from your own content, and they go out at the scheduled time. Nothing is published until you've approved it.

For example: an article or a webinar turned into posts for LinkedIn and Instagram, with suggested replies to comments and a monthly report.

02

Website design

A website that loads quickly, that you can update yourself and that leads people to get in touch. From day one it is connected to your forms, your booking diary or your CRM.

For example: a services website with appointment booking, a form that arrives sorted in your inbox, basic SEO and legal texts.

03

Reviews and online reputation

We ask for a Google review after each service and draft replies for you to check before they're published.

For example: an automatic message at the end of each appointment and an alert when a negative review comes in.

04

Automated email marketing

Welcome emails that go out on their own and a newsletter for different groups of clients, with their opens and clicks. Unsubscribes and bounces are handled automatically, as GDPR requires.

For example: three emails over the two weeks after someone gets in touch, ending with an invitation to a meeting.

05

Lead generation with a downloadable resource

A guide or a pack your clients download by leaving their email. The contact goes into your CRM and gets a few emails that end with an invitation to a meeting.

For example: "A guide to buying your first home" for an estate agency, or "The self-employed tax calendar" for an accountancy firm.

Advice and training

For when you don't know where to start, or you want your team to use AI without getting into trouble with data.

01

Process diagnostic

We look at how your business works and which programs you use, and give you a list of what can be simplified, in order of priority.

From €190, which we deduct if we then do the project together.

02

Getting your AI working

Many businesses pay for Copilot, Gemini or ChatGPT and barely use it. We set it up with your documents and show your team how to use it in their work.

For example: internal assistants that know your documentation, and prompt templates for each role.

03

Training and responsible AI

Hands-on sessions with your team, a guide to which data can be used and an internal usage policy. This covers the training duty in the EU AI Act.

For example: a one-page guide for the whole team and a session with cases from your industry.
