What takes up the most time in a clinic

The phone interrupts sessions

Appointments are booked by phone and, if nobody picks up, the patient tries another clinic.

Patients book online at any time and choose the practitioner and the reason for the visit.

Empty slots

Some patients forget their appointment or cancel the day before.

They get a reminder to confirm and, if someone cancels, the slot is offered to the waiting list.

Paperwork at the first visit

The patient form, consent and data protection notice are filled in on paper, eating into session time.

The patient fills it in and signs it on their phone before they arrive.

Unfinished treatments

Some patients stop coming as soon as they feel better.

Exercises arrive on their phone, we ask how they're feeling and we let you know if someone stops booking.

Frequently asked questions

Got another question? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

Is it compatible with health data protection?

Yes. Health data has special protection, so we sign the data processing agreement, keep the data in the EU and limit who has access. The AI only receives what's strictly needed for each task.

Does the AI make diagnoses?

No. At most, it summarises what the patient has written so the practitioner can prepare for the appointment. Assessment and treatment are always decided by the practitioner.

Do I have to change my booking software?

Not necessarily. If your software allows connections, we work with it. If not, we build a simple custom booking diary.

How do WhatsApp reminders work?

We use the official WhatsApp business version, with messages approved by Meta. The patient confirms, changes or cancels the appointment from the same message.
