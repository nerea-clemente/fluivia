Fluivia for clinics

Fewer phone calls and fewer empty slots in your clinic

We set up an online diary so your patients can book, confirm and change their appointment on their phone. Your team has the diary, the patient records and each patient's follow-up in one place.

What's included

01

Online booking

Patients book at any time in a diary linked to yours, choosing the practitioner and the reason for the visit.

Before the appointment they get a reminder to confirm, change or cancel, and if someone cancels, we let the waiting list know.

02

Patient form and consent

Before the first visit, the patient fills in their form and signs the consent and privacy notice on their phone.

The practitioner gets a summary with the important points flagged, like allergies or medication, and no session time is lost on paperwork.

03

Treatment follow-up

Each patient has their exercise videos, reminders and a log of how they're feeling on their phone.

If someone stops booking halfway through treatment, we let you know so you can call them.

04

Questions and reviews

An assistant answers questions about opening hours, prices and how to prepare for the appointment, and passes on to reception anything a person needs to see.

When patients are discharged, we ask for a Google review with a direct link and draft replies for you.

Frequently asked questions

Is it compatible with health data protection?

Yes. Health data has special protection, so we sign the data processing agreement, keep the data in the EU and limit who has access. The AI only receives what's strictly needed for each task.

Does the AI make diagnoses?

No. At most, it summarises what the patient has written so the practitioner can prepare for the appointment. Assessment and treatment are always decided by the practitioner.

Do I have to change my booking software?

Not necessarily. If your software allows connections, we work with it. If not, we build a simple custom booking diary.

How do WhatsApp reminders work?

We use the official WhatsApp business version, with messages approved by Meta. The patient confirms, changes or cancels the appointment from the same message.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we include. As a guide, online booking starts at €390 to set up and €49 a month, and the enquiries assistant at €690 and €89 a month. Patient records and follow-up are built to measure. Prices exclude VAT, and the proposal also sets out licences and maintenance.

Who helps me if something goes wrong?

We do. Write to us at info@fluivia.ai and we look at it the same working day. The monthly fee includes maintenance and small adjustments.

How do your patients book now?

Tell us what you use and what you'd like to improve. If you like, we can talk it through on a call and tell you what would make sense for your clinic.
